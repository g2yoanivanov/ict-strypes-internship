# Blackjack Rules

## Card Values

- Number cards (2-10): worth their face value.
- Jack, Queen, King: worth **10** each.
- Ace: worth **1 or 11**, whichever benefits the hand more.

### Examples
- A + 7 = 18 ("soft 18")
- A + 7 + 9 = 17 (Ace becomes 1)

---

## Game Setup

1. Player places a bet.
2. Dealer gives:
   - Two cards to each player.
   - Two cards to themselves.
3. Usually:
   - One dealer card is face up ("upcard").
   - One dealer card is face down ("hole card").

---

## Objective

You win if:
- Your hand is higher than the dealer's hand without exceeding 21.
- The dealer busts and you do not.
- You have a blackjack and the dealer does not.

You lose if:
- You bust.
- The dealer has a higher hand.
- The dealer has blackjack and you do not.

A tie is called a **push** and your bet is returned.

---

## Natural Blackjack

A blackjack is:
- An Ace + any 10-value card as the first two cards dealt.

Examples:
- A + K
- A + Q
- A + J
- A + 10

Typically pays **3:2** (some casinos pay 6:5).

If both player and dealer have blackjack:
- Push (tie).

---

# Player Actions

## 1. Hit

Take another card.

You may continue hitting until:
- You stand.
- You bust.

## 2. Stand

Take no more cards.

Your turn ends immediately.

## 3. Double Down

You:
- Double your original bet.
- Receive exactly one more card.
- Must then stand.

## 4. Surrender (If Offered)

You:
- Give up the hand immediately.
- Lose only half your bet.

Usually available only before taking another action.

---

# Dealer Rules

Dealers must follow fixed rules.

## Dealer Must Hit

Dealer must hit on:
- 16 or less

## Dealer Must Stand

Dealer must stand on:
- 17 or more

## Soft 17 Rule

A soft 17 contains an Ace counted as 11.

Examples:
- A + 6 = soft 17

Two common casino variants:

### S17
Dealer stands on soft 17.

### H17
Dealer hits on soft 17.

---

## Busting

A hand busts when its value exceeds 21.

Examples:
- 10 + 8 + 5 = 23
- K + Q + 2 = 22

If the player busts:
- The player loses immediately.

---

## End of Round

1. Dealer reveals the hole card.
2. Dealer follows mandatory hit/stand rules.
3. Hands are compared.

### Outcomes

- Higher hand (without busting) wins.
- Equal totals result in a push.
- Dealer bust means remaining players win.

---

# Basic Strategy Tips

- Always split Aces and 8s.
- Never split 10s.
- Stand on hard 17 or higher.
- Double down on 11 when allowed.
- Insurance is generally not recommended.

---

# Example Round

1. Player receives 10 + 6 = 16.
2. Dealer shows 10.
3. Player hits.
4. Player receives 4, making 20.
5. Player stands.
6. Dealer reveals 7 under the 10, totaling 17.
7. Dealer stands.
8. Player's 20 beats dealer's 17.

**Result: Player wins.**
